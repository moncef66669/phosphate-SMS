1. Context

The phosphate planning boundary is the complete three-site value chain, not M16 alone. Louta, Benguerir and MPH are peer controlled yards in Domain A; each has an SMS and receives one coordinated seven-day plan containing hourly jobs. An optional rolling mode refines the first four hours to 15-minute decisions and retains an hourly tail; timed asset changes and issued jobs keep exact-minute boundaries. Mining and washing upstream of the stockyards are interfaces, not directly controlled plant processes; the S0 simulator nevertheless represents selected Louta and Benguerir campaigns as auditable process-transfer stages so that ore placement, reclaim, recovery, product stacking and corridor/train withdrawals conserve mass. Domain B plans M17 sulphur independently on the same platform.

5.1 Repère position model

Each stockpile uses the operating scale R0–R1000, representing 1,000 equal longitudinal intervals. A job may therefore state “store this grade on LT-P1 from R400 to R600.” Repères are operator-facing; physical metres remain canonical for SMS/ASP exchange, inventory segments, machine travel and safety distances:

x_m = x₀_m + (R / 1000) × (x₁_m − x₀_m)  ·  R = 1000 × (x_m − x₀_m) / (x₁_m − x₀_m)

The Innomotics FRS confirms numeric Start/End positions (StartX/StopX), per-metre pile inventory and position-range validation. The term repère and the R0–R1000 scale are a project-team operating convention confirmed 24-Jul-2026, not a claim that every pile is 1,000 m long. Before live use, each pile still needs its R0 endpoint and increasing direction, surveyed physical origin/usable length, and the ICD's StartX/StopX units and scale.

Deployment: dedicated L3 application on controlled OT; business exchanges cross a managed DMZ. Missing/stale SMS data are flagged with last-good-state plus operator fallback. Configured: sites, assets, reachability, route graph, specs, compatibility, patterns, weights, calendars, endpoints and IDs.

All equipment, conveyor and stockpile capacities and rates are represented in physical/as-handled tonnes at their material stage. TSM is a reported merchant-dry equivalent, not the capacity basis: raw physical mass converts through raw moisture and beneficiation yield; wet washed-product physical mass retains product moisture; after drying, 1 physical dry tonne = 1 TSM. The unified Grade configuration page maintains chemistry, yield/moisture, material profiles and receiving limits, and supports persistent add/delete with referential checks.

Reclaim take-off — two separate streams: each reclaimer feeds its own T11 → T11S magnetic separator → T12 (belt scale) → hopper M16A-A-R01 with air blasters → its own clamshell + rod gate bank (M16A-A-H01…H04 / M16A-B-H01…H04) → T13 outbound belt at 1,500 TPH. That chain is confirmed equipment. What is not confirmed is the destination: neither the PFD nor PRO-0001 says which gate or downstream diverter sends a stream to PAP rather than to drying, and the confluence point is not resolvable from the available sheet. The model therefore separates the two — a confirmed gate-bank link per stream, then a derived/TBC route selection toward PAP (six parallel 1,500 TPH conveyors, 3 running + 3 future) or drying (two parallel 1,299 TPH conveyors, both out of scope). One destination per bank per bucket is derived from the single outbound belt; the documented 10-minute changeover is recorded but not modelled.

Reception envelope — one current path: PRO-0001 §2.1.1 places auto sampler M16VP01 on M16A-A-T01 and M16VP02 on “the adjacent future conveyor”, and the PFD marks that line FUTURE. C1 and C2 therefore share one current 2,600 TPH reception path: aggregate reception is capped at 2,600 TPH, never 2 × 2,600 TPH, and the initial in-transit profile shows the corridors running alternately rather than both at full rate in the same hour.

What is confirmed and what is not: the eight 125,000 t hangars, the four-bay/machine-line sequence, the machine identities, their lines and their rates are confirmed by Q80130-MZI-M16-ME-PFD-0001[3] Rev 3. The hangar names inside a bay are inferred — the PFD does not name them, so HP-01…08 remains a working convention pending the plot plan and the official SMS pile-ID register. Metric positions stay schematic: the PFD is a flow sheet, not to scale. Machine-to-bay assignment applies the project-team rule that a machine translating between adjacent bays serves those bays only; per-line travel limits still require a surveyed GA.

13.7 Current implementation baseline — September 2026

This subsection records the current application behaviour and supersedes any earlier prototype wording in this scope that implies a single-day horizon, fixed one-hour job duration, a separate contingency-entry table or direct two-stream reclaim blending.

AreaImplemented behaviour
Planning envelopeDefault tactical horizon is 7 days / 168 hourly decisions. Optional rolling refinement uses 15-minute decisions in the first four hours and hourly decisions thereafter. Outage, derate and execution boundaries retain exact minutes, and adjacent compatible slices are merged into one continuous job rather than displayed as artificial one-hour jobs.
DemandOne dated seven-day table covers Benguerir, Louta and MPH. A unit may have multiple grade/quantity campaigns on the same day; each row carries an intraday start/end and priority. Demand is retained when a route is unavailable and is reported as unmet rather than silently removed.
Routing and contingenciesPlan → Routing availability reuses the Monitor's global flow diagram with All/Louta/Benguerir/MPH filters. Clicking a planner-controlled machine, conveyor, route or plant opens one harmonised control: Available / Disabled / Future, plus a physical-rate cap, applied for the whole run or an exact timed interval. This consolidates planned contingencies; a separate table-entry page is no longer required. Current state is explicit on the icon/line and in the consolidated control list.
ConfigurationThe simplified Configure section includes one Grade configuration page with Chemical grades, Yield & moisture, Material profiles and Receiving limits. Grade records can be added or deleted persistently; a referenced grade must first be remapped, preventing orphaned material profiles or stockpile assignments. The user-facing nominal grade name is BT. PAP-2 exposes the same three lifecycle states as every destination and defaults to Future.
Mass and quality basisCapacities, inventories, rates, jobs and blend tonnes are physical/as-handled values at the applicable stage. Raw physical mass → TSM uses (1 − raw moisture) × beneficiation yield; wet washed product retains configured moisture; after drying, 1 physical dry tonne = 1 TSM. Chemistry of a blend is dry-solids weighted, while moisture is physical-mass weighted. TSM appears as a secondary label/hover value only when a valid grade conversion exists.
Integrated mimic and inspectionThe same diagram is used for planning and monitoring. Reduced labels preserve legibility while machines, plants and conveyors carry prominent state cues. Stockpile fill is drawn proportional to physical occupancy; hover exposes stockpile grade composition, physical mass, capacity/fill, stage and applicable TSM equivalent.
Job schedule and resultsMonitor exposes readiness, demand/service and unmet quantity, quality/off-spec/give-away, inventory/safety/capacity, route and machine utilisation/coverage, positioning/conflict, and replanning/recovery evidence. It also shows the active configurable objective weights. Schedule filters include site/domain, machine, operation and status plus Displayed period = full run or an exact calendar day.
M16 blendingThe implemented point is the M16 humid stockpile HP-07 before its PAP receiving campaign. The dedicated Blending page offers Blend matrix (operator-set fixed shares) and Optimized shares (component masses are solver variables). In optimized mode composition, stacking time, routes, equipment calendars, source inventory, competing work and destination interval are solved together in physical tonnes. The proposal may change on replan until stacking starts; executed stacking locks the resulting matrix. Reclaim cannot start before the configured pile-ready boundary, and stacked batch mass must equal matrix mass exactly.

The following blending extensions are not yet implemented: Benguerir washed-product piles into a train campaign; multi-quality raw-stock feed into washing lines; and spatial tracking of layers/zones and their changing reclaim composition. Grade-class chemistry is currently configured as vectors plus destination receiving intervals; defining the grades themselves entirely by min/max characteristic intervals remains a planned modelling extension.

Rev AC verification history: the focused blending/scheduling regression set passes 61 tests. A full seven-day physical solve has also returned a feasible 12,000 t HP-07 campaign with jointly selected 1,000 t TBT + 11,000 t BT-LOW-CD and no reclaim before the 48-hour ready boundary. These are deterministic simulator checks, not proof of plant performance. The wider legacy repository suite still contains stale UI/report expectations and is not yet a clean acceptance suite; operational validation continues to require governed SMS/SQMS data, approved quality intervals, route evidence and shadow/pilot comparison.

Balanced, Quality and Inventory continuity weight profiles are optional, uncalibrated sensitivities.
Independent SMS comparisonA synthetic 12-hour three-site case uses three deterministic local protocols, equal nominations and the same physical replay as the coordinated plan. There is zero demonstrated throughput uplift in this case; neither staging nor annual savings are claimed for the actual plant.

These arms share the global feasibility scheduler and therefore do not isolate full-plant information blindness. Failed solves or execution checks cannot support monetary benefit.
