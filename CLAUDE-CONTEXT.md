## 1. The project in one paragraph

The phosphate project is an end-to-end **three-site planning system**, not an M16-only project with mine interfaces: **Louta mine stockyard + SMS feeds the MPH M16 hub through C1, and Benguerir mine stockyard + SMS feeds M16 through C2**. Louta, Benguerir and M16 are peer controlled sites in Domain A; the layer issues jobs to all three SMS and coordinates mine inventory/dispatch, corridor delay and M16 reception/blending/reclaim as one plan. Between the weekly/monthly production plan and SMS execution, the application **automates routine quality, quantity, placement, blending and sequencing plan-authoring under operator supervision**, producing a continuously re-planned hourly job plan. It is a dedicated L3/MES-level application—not a new SMS—acting as the automated PlanAuthor while operators retain Accept/Reject and override authority. M17 sulphur is a separate planning domain on the same platform.
